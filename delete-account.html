Delete your account

Lunacy Depths

You can permanently delete your Lunacy Depths online account and the data stored with it.

In the game

  1. Open the game and tap the profile button (top right), then Settings.
  2. Under Online, tap Delete my online account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Without the game

Message the team in our Discord server (discord.gg/ZjWRgAK9Yh) with your hero name or friend code, and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account, leaderboard entry, cloud save, friends list, clan membership, Discord link and activity log. If you led a clan, leadership passes to another member; a clan with no members left is deleted. Nothing is kept.

What stays

Your hero on your own device is not touched. Clan chat messages you sent earlier may stay in that clan's history.
