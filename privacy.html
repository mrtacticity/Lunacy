Privacy policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

Lunacy Depths is a free idle dungeon game made by a small independent team. This page explains what the game stores and why. We do not sell data, show ads or use tracking or analytics services.

Playing offline

Your hero and progress are saved on your own device. Nothing leaves your device unless online features are on.

What online features store

  • Your hero name and a random account ID and friend code.
  • Game progress used for leaderboards and profiles: deepest floor, levels, boss and raid kills, collection, achievements, title and the look of your gear.
  • A cloud copy of your save, so you can continue on another device.
  • Clans and friends: which clan you are in, clan chat messages you send, and your friends list.
  • Discord, only if you link it: your Discord user ID and username. We use them to give rank roles in our Discord server and to log you in. The game asks Discord only to identify you and to add you to our server.
  • A short activity log (for example rare drops, gifts from the team and suspicious progress) used to run announcements and stop cheating.

Data is stored with Cloudflare, which runs our server. A sign-in token is kept on your device so the game knows it is you.

Public information

Leaderboards and profiles show your hero name and progress. You can make your profile private in the game, which shows you as "Anonymous Adventurer".

Deleting your data

You can delete your online account at any time. See how to delete your account.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
