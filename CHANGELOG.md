# Lunacy Depths Changelog

New entries go at the TOP. The Discord bot posts the newest entry every time this file is pushed.

## v1.24 | The Raid Update | Oct 31, 2026
- Raids are rebuilt from the ground up! Every raid is now a walkable boss arena: watch for red warning zones and walk out of them before they land. Auto-hunt still attacks, but it will not dodge for you
- Each raid has three phases with new mechanics: shields you break by killing adds, homing orbs, sweeping beams, lava lines, rising tides and arrow rain you can only survive behind a pillar
- New: The Gilded Gallery (unlocks at floor 250). Vaelith, the Gilded Huntress keeps her distance and rains arrows. Drops the Huntress set, Vaelith's Longbow, the Gilded Arbalest and the Gilded Pup pet
- New: Sanctum of the Hollow Star (floor 400). Astraeus teleports, fires chasing star orbs and sweeping beams. Drops the Starweaver set, the Staff of Astraeus, the Shard of the Star wand and the Starling pet
- New: The Abyssal Maw (floor 600). Cut its three glowing tentacles to open its mouth, then strike while it is exposed. Drops the Abyssal set, the Trident of the Maw, the Reaper of the Deep and the Lil' Maw pet
- The Hollow Court and Throne of Ash are reworked into the new arena style
- Raids are graded S to D on speed and hits taken. Better grades raise your unique drop chance (S is ×1.6)
- One Second Wind per raid: the first time you fall, you get back up with half your health
- Ranged and magic players finally have their own raid weapons, with brand new artwork
- Raid clears on leaderboards and profiles now count all five raids
- New website pages: a privacy policy and how to delete your account
- You can now delete your online account in Settings → Online
- Discord: the role buttons in #get-roles work again, and the new /lunacy command opens a menu with leaderboards, your hero, clans and game stats

## v1.23 | Fair views, floor picker and Cursed Descent | Oct 30, 2026
- Fixed: swords, spears, maces and scythes had a gap where the handle should be (most visible in the bag and trophy room). Every weapon is one piece again
- Classic and Explore views are now balanced: Classic adds a short walk between fights to match Explore, so neither view pushes faster
- Choose a floor: tap the dungeon name on the battle screen to jump back to any floor you have reached and farm it
- New: Cursed Descent. Stake 20% of your gold and push 10 floors under a curse (Shadowed, Blighted or Damned). Tougher monsters, much better loot, and clearing all 10 floors multiplies your stake and gives boss chests. Die and the stake is lost. Find it in the floor menu
- If you die on a floor that is far too strong for you, you now fall back to a floor you can actually farm, and the message tells you to get stronger first
- Rankings on player profiles now load properly
- Rare drops and new pets are announced in the Discord drops channel (once the team turns it on)
- Gifts from the Lunacy team now arrive in game with a message
